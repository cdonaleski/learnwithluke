♾️ Infinity

How it works

Infinity is not a number. It is the idea of something that never ends — you can never count to it or reach it, however long you go on.

The four parts here are real mathematics. Hilbert’s Hotel is a puzzle the mathematician David Hilbert used in 1924; the pairing idea is how Georg Cantor compared infinities in the 1870s and 1890s.

Four ways to poke at something that never ends.

Is there a biggest number?

Build the biggest number you can. Whatever you make, watch what happens.

Your number

And one bigger

Whatever number you build, adding 1 makes a bigger one — every time, forever. So there is no biggest number. That is what infinity means: not a giant number at the end, but the fact that there is no end.

Hilbert’s Hotel

This hotel has infinitely many rooms — 1, 2, 3, 4, and on forever. Tonight every room is full. Can it fit anyone else?
