How to Use It

Moving the hands
Press anywhere on the clock and drag. It takes hold of whichever hand you were nearest. Swing the long hand all the way round and watch the short one move on by one hour — the two are joined, and that is why.
Why the hour hand is never quite on the number
At half past three it sits halfway between the 3 and the 4, because half the hour has gone. Most drawings of clocks get this wrong and leave it sitting on the 3, which is why so many children read half past three as half past four.
Why 3:45 is "quarter to four"
Once you are past the half hour you stop counting from the hour you are in and start counting to the next one. The clock still shows 3, but there are fifteen minutes to go until 4, so it is quarter to four. This is the single most confusing thing about clocks and it is worth saying out loud a few times.
The three things you can do
Have a play — move the hands and read what it is called. Read the clock — it shows a time, you pick the words. Set the clock — it gives you the words, you move the hands.
How hard
Start at o'clock and half past. Then quarters, then every five minutes, then any minute at all. There is no hurry — quarters alone will get you through most of a school year.
