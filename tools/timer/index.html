    How to Use It

    Countdown
    Pick one of the preset times or type your own, then press Start. When it reaches zero the ring empties and a chime plays.
    Stopwatch
    Switch to ⏱️ Stopwatch to count upwards instead. Press 🚩 Lap to record a split without stopping the clock.
    Keyboard
    Space starts and pauses, R resets, and L records a lap.
    Pausing
    Pause keeps your place — pressing Start again picks up exactly where you left off, not from the beginning.
    Switching tabs
    The timer follows the real clock rather than counting frames, so it stays accurate even if you switch to another tab and come back. The chime only sounds once you return, though.
