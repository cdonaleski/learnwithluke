    How to Use It

    Flip
    Tap the coin, press the Flip button, or hit Space. Heads is the crown, tails is the lion.
    The tally
    Every flip is counted, and the last 50 are shown as a row of H and T chips. Your tally is saved on this device, so it's still there tomorrow. Clear Tally starts again from zero.
    Best run
    The longest streak of the same side in a row. Runs of four or five are much more common than people expect!
    Try this
    Flip × 10 and look at the Heads %. It's often nowhere near 50 — maybe 80% or 30%. Now press × 1000 a few times and watch the bar settle close to half and half. A coin has no memory, but the more you flip, the closer the average gets to fair. That's the law of large numbers.
    Is it really fair?
    Yes — each flip is an independent 50/50, and nothing is nudged by what came before. A long run of heads does not make tails "due" next.
