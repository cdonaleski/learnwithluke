What is on it

One on each line. Up to 24 of them.

How to Use It

Spinning
Press Spin, tap the wheel, or hit Space.
Is it fair?
Yes. Every slice has exactly the same chance, however big it looks — and the slices are all the same size anyway. The winner is picked first and the wheel is aimed at that slice, so where the pointer stops and what it says can never disagree.
Going round everybody
Tick take the winner off and each name comes off as it is picked, so nobody gets two turns before everybody has had one. Put everybody back starts again.
Ideas for it
Names for whose turn it is. Jobs, so nobody argues about who got the bins. Dinners, when nobody can decide. Or numbers, letters, or yes / no / ask again later.
It remembers
Your list stays on this device — not sent anywhere, and not shared with anyone else's.
