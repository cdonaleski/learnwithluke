🔒 Privacy

The short version: the games, puzzles and tools collect nothing about you at all. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The only accounts on this site are for an invitation-only after-homeschool club, set up by hand, and for a child only with a parent's consent. Managing screen time is a parent's job, not this site's — it does not know who is here, and does not want to.

For parents

Worth saying plainly, because it is the sort of thing that is usually buried in small print nobody reads.

⏰ Screen time is yours to manage

This site does not watch the clock, set limits, or tell anybody how long a child has been here — and it could not, because it does not know who is here. Deciding how much time your child spends on a screen is a parent's job, and we would not want it any other way.

🧒 Made by a boy, with his parents

Everything here started as something Luke was interested in — a cube he wanted to solve, a game he wanted to make. He builds it with his parents' help, and they check what goes on it. It is a family project that other children are welcome to use.

📚 For learning, and for fun

The site is meant for education and enjoyment. It is not a curriculum, not a substitute for a teacher, and not assessed by anybody. Nothing here is a qualification.

👀 Have a look round yourself

You are very welcome to. There is nothing hidden from parents: no chat, no messaging, no way for a stranger to contact a child, and nowhere for a child to type something that anybody else will see.

We take real care with what goes on here, and we check it works. But it is made by a family in their spare time and given away free, so it comes with no promises beyond that — if something is wrong or broken, tell us and we will fix it.

What we do not do

📊 No analytics

Nothing counts visitors, records what you click, or reports back to anybody. There is no Google Analytics, no pixel, no tag manager.

📣 No adverts

None, and no advertising networks either — which is what usually does the tracking on a children's site.

🍪 No cookies for visitors

Browse, play and leave without a single cookie being set. Signed-in club members are the one exception, explained below.

🤝 Nothing sold or shared

There is nothing to sell. No data is passed to anybody, for any purpose, ever.

Your scores stay on your own device

High scores, settings, which puzzles you have finished, which cube algorithms you have ticked off — all of it is kept by your own browser, on your own computer. It is never sent anywhere, and we cannot see it.

That also means it is yours to delete. Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes every trace of it, and nothing here will notice or mind.

The one thing loaded from elsewhere

Being straight about this: the site's lettering comes from Google Fonts. That means your browser asks Google's servers for the font files, and Google can see the request — your rough location from your IP address, and which browser you use. No cookie is set and nothing identifies you, but it is a request to a company that is not us, and you deserve to know it happens rather than find out later.

Nothing else is loaded from anywhere else. No scripts, no images, no embedded videos that play on their own.

Club accounts

One corner of this site — the speedcube club — is for members of an invitation-only after-homeschool club, and needs signing in to. Everything else is open to anybody with no account at all.

🚫 Nobody can sign themselves up

There is no "create an account" button anywhere, and self sign-up is switched off. Accounts exist only because the club organiser made one, by hand, for somebody he knows.

👪 A parent says yes first

An account for a child is only created after their parent or guardian has agreed to it, in person or by message. No child is signed up on their own say-so.

📇 What an account holds

An email address to sign in with, a display name, and cube competition times that are already published by the World Cube Association. No addresses, no phone numbers, no photographs.

🗑️ Ask and it is gone

Any parent may ask for their child's account and everything in it to be deleted, at any time, without giving a reason. It will be done, and it will not be kept in a backup afterwards.

Account details are held by Supabase, a hosting company, on our behalf. Signing in stores a token in your browser so you are not asked again on every page. That token is the only cookie-like thing this site ever keeps, it belongs to the club pages, and signing out removes it.

Questions, and asking for things to be removed

Who runs this
Learn With Luke is made by a dad for his son, and shared because other children might like it too. It is not a company and it does not make money.
How to get in touch
Club members have the organiser's contact details on the club page. Anybody else with a question about privacy — including a parent who wants an account removed — can reach him through the club page or by asking any club member to pass it on.
Children's privacy
This site is meant for children, which is exactly why it collects nothing from them. There is no profile to build, because nothing is gathered to build one from. Where an account does exist, it is because a parent asked for it.
If any of this changes
If the site ever starts collecting something it does not collect today, this page will say so before it happens, not afterwards.
