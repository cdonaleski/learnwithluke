Haven't got an account?

There is no sign-up
Accounts are made by hand, for people the club organiser knows, and for a child only after a parent has said yes. That is deliberate: it means nobody can quietly create one.
Forgotten the password
Ask the club organiser and he will set a new one. There is no automatic reset, because that would need a working email inbox and most of the members are children.
What signing in stores
A token in this browser, so you are not asked again on every page. Signing out removes it. The privacy page explains the rest.
