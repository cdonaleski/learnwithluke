How to Play

Make a guess
Tap a colour to drop it into the highlighted slot, or tap a slot first to choose where it goes. On a keyboard, 1–8 pick colours, arrows move between slots, Backspace clears one and Enter checks.
Reading the clues
Each guess gets a set of small dots. A black dot means one peg is the right colour in the right place. A white dot means a peg is the right colour but in the wrong place. The dots don't tell you which pegs — that's the puzzle!
Important
Each peg only ever earns one dot. If the code has one red and you guess three reds, you get one dot for red, not three.
Difficulty
Easy is 4 pegs from 6 colours with no repeats and 12 tries. Medium allows repeated colours. Hard is 5 pegs from 8 colours with repeats and 10 tries.
Tip
Start by trying a row of one colour, then a row of another. That tells you exactly how many of each colour the code contains before you worry about where they go.
