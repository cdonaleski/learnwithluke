How to Play

Steer
Press the arrow keys or W A S D. On a tablet or phone, swipe across the board or tap the arrow buttons.
Grow
Every apple adds 10 points and makes you two segments longer. The longer you get, the trickier it is to find room.
Pause
Press Space, tap the board, or use the Pause button.
Walls
On Solid, touching the edge ends the run. On Pass through, you slide out one side and reappear on the other — much friendlier while you're learning.
Best score
Each speed keeps its own best score, saved on this device. Changing speed or walls starts a fresh game.
