How to Play

Dropping
The block slides back and forth across the top. Tap the tower, press the Drop button, or hit Space to let it go.
The one rule
Only the part sitting on the block below stays. The bit hanging over the edge is sliced off and tumbles away — and your next block is that bit narrower.
Perfect drops
Line it up almost exactly and it snaps flush without losing any width at all. Do it several times in a row and each one is worth more.
Getting harder
Nothing changes the difficulty except you. Every sloppy drop narrows the tower, and the block speeds up as you climb. It ends when the tower gets too narrow to hit.
The dotted lines
Those show the exact edges of the block below. Watch the lines rather than the block — it's much easier to judge.
Tip
Don't drop at the moment it looks right — drop a fraction early. The block is still moving while it falls, and everyone is late at first.
