🚀 Asteroids

The 1979 arcade classic, just like the original. Turn with ← →, thrust with ↑, and shoot with Space. Big rocks break into smaller ones — clear them all to reach the next wave!

Asteroids game

Press Start to launch!

How to Play

Steer
← → (or A D) spin your ship. There is no brake — in space you keep drifting, so thrust the other way to slow down!
Thrust
↑ (or W) fires your engine.
Shoot
Space fires. Only four shots can be in flight at once, so make them count.
Hyperspace
H or Shift jumps you somewhere else on the screen — a great escape when you're surrounded.
Pause
P or the Pause button.
Fly off the edge
Everything wraps around — leave the right side and you reappear on the left.
Points
Big rock 20 · medium rock 50 · small rock 100 · big saucer 200 · small saucer 1000. You get an extra ship every 10,000 points.
Watch out
Saucers shoot back. The small one aims right at you!
