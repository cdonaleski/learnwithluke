🐸 Frogger

Get five frogs safely home to the lily pads. Dodge the traffic, then ride the logs across the river. Careful — on the road you must touch nothing, but on the river you must touch something!

How to Play

Hopping
Arrow keys or W A S D. On a tablet, swipe on the board or use the arrow buttons. A tap on the board hops you forward.
The road
Touch a car and you're squashed. Wait in the gaps between lanes — you don't have to cross in one go.
The river
This is the opposite! Water is deadly, so you must land on a log or a turtle. Once you're on one it carries you along, so jump off before it reaches the edge.
Getting home
Five lily pads at the top, and each one takes a frog. Land on a pad and you start again from the bottom with the next frog. Fill all five and the whole thing starts over, faster.
Scoring
10 points every time you get a row further than you've been on that life, 60 for getting a frog home, and 250 for filling all five pads.
Tip
On the river, hop onto the back end of a log rather than the front. It buys you time to look for the next one before your ride runs out.
