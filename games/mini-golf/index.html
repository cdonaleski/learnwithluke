⛳ Mini Golf

Nine holes. Get the ball in the cup in as few shots as you can. Drag from the ball in the direction you want it to go — the further you drag, the harder you hit it.

    How to Play

    Taking a shot
    Press on the ball and drag in the direction you want it to go — towards the hole, not away from it. A dotted line shows your aim and the little bar shows how hard you'll hit it. Let go to putt.
    How hard?
    The bar goes green, then yellow, then red. Red is a very big hit — useful for a long straight hole, and usually a mistake anywhere near the cup.
    Par
    Par is how many shots a hole should take. Finishing in fewer is under par, which is good. Your total across all nine is what counts, and the leaderboard keeps the lowest.
    Bouncing
    The ball bounces off the wooden walls, losing a little speed each time. On the trickier holes bouncing off a wall on purpose is much easier than trying to curve round.
    Rimming out
    Hit it too hard and the ball will run straight over the cup instead of dropping in. If you keep skipping over the hole, try it gentler.
    Stuck?
    After ten shots on one hole the ball is picked up and you move on. Replay Hole starts the current hole again and forgets what it cost you.
