How to Play

The rule
Every row, every column and every three-by-three box must contain the numbers 1 to 9, each one exactly once. That's the whole game.
Filling squares
Tap a square to select it, then tap a number — or just press a number key. Backspace or ⌫ clears it. Arrow keys move around the grid.
Notes
Turn on ✏️ Notes (or press N) to pencil in small numbers when you know a square is one of two or three possibilities. Filling the square in properly clears its notes.
Helpful highlights
Selecting a square shades its row, column and box, and every other square holding the same number lights up. Wrong numbers turn red straight away, so a slip never spoils the whole puzzle.
Every puzzle has one answer
Clues are removed one at a time, and any removal that would leave the puzzle with more than one possible answer is put straight back. So the puzzle can always be reasoned out — you never have to guess and hope.
Tip
Look for the row, column or box that already has the most numbers in it. The fewer squares left, the fewer things they can be.
