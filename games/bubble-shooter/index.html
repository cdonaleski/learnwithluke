How to Play

Aim and shoot
Move the mouse or your finger to aim — the dotted line shows where the bubble will go. Click or tap to fire. On a keyboard, ← and → aim and Space shoots.
Popping
Land a bubble so that three or more of the same kind are touching and the whole group pops. Any bubbles left dangling with nothing above them fall too — those are worth double.
Bank shots
Bubbles bounce off the left and right walls, so you can curl a shot around the side of the stack to reach the back.
Swap
Not keen on the bubble you've got? Press S or the Swap button to switch it with the next one.
The drop
Every 8 shots a fresh row appears at the top and pushes everything down — Math Mode gives you 12, since working out a sum takes longer. The Until drop counter shows how many you have left. If the stack crosses the red line the run is over, so pop the bottom rows to push it back up.
Math Mode
Your bubble shows a sum like 5 + 3 instead of a colour, and every bubble on the board has a number on it. Work out the answer and shoot at three matching numbers. Pick Adding, Add & Subtract, Bigger Sums or Times Tables.
Grown-ups: adding a maths level
Open games/bubble-shooter/js/math-levels.js and copy one of the blocks. Give it an id, name, icon, exactly six different values, and a problem function that returns a sum working out to the value it's given. It appears in the Maths row straight away.
