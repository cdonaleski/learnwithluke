How to Play

Move your paddle
Slide your mouse or finger up and down the court, or press W / S (↑ / ↓ also work in 1 player mode).
Player 2
In 2 player mode, the player on the right uses the ↑ and ↓ arrow keys.
Pause
Press Space or the Pause button any time. Press it again to keep playing.
Scoring
You score a point when the ball goes past the other paddle. First player to reach 7 points wins the match.
Tip
Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to send it flying at a sharper angle — and the ball speeds up a little with every hit!
