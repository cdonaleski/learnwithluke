    How to Play

    Place your fleet
    Tap a square in your waters to drop the next ship there — the square you tap is its top or left end. Use Across / Down (or press R) to turn it, or press Place For Me to scatter the whole fleet at random.
    Your ships
    Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3) and Destroyer (2). Ships can't overlap, but they may sit right next to each other.
    Firing
    Tap a square in the enemy waters. A red square is a hit, a pale one is a miss. Sink every enemy ship to win — but the computer is firing back at your fleet each turn too.
    How the computer plays
    It fires more or less at random until it hits something, then works outwards from that hit. Once it lands two hits in a row it knows which way the ship is lying and follows the line.
    Tip
    Every ship is at least two squares long, so you never need to fire at two squares side by side — checking every other square still finds every ship, in half the shots.
