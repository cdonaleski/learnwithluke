How to Play

Find a pair
Click or tap a card to turn it over, then turn over a second one. If the two pictures match they stay face up. If not, they flip back — so try to remember where they were!
Moves
Every second card you turn over counts as one move. Fewer moves is better, and your best score for each board size is saved on this device.
Themes
Pick Animals, Food, Space, Ocean, Vehicles or Shapes. Changing the theme deals a fresh set of cards, and your choice is remembered next time.
Board size
Start with 🐣 Easy (6 pairs) and work up to 🦅 Hard (12 pairs) once you've got the hang of it.
Grown-ups: adding a theme
Open games/memory/js/themes.js and copy one of the blocks. Give it an id, a name, an icon and a list of cards — 12 different ones unlocks every board size. It appears in the Theme row straight away; nothing else needs changing.
