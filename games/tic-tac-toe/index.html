How to Play

Take a turn
Click or tap any empty square. ✖️ always goes first in a round, and who opens swaps each new round so it's fair.
Winning
Three of your marks in a line — a row, a column, or a diagonal. The winning three light up.
Computer skill
Easy plays almost at random and often misses a block. Medium plays well but slips up now and then. Hard plays perfectly — the very best you can do is a draw.
Two players
Switch to 👫 2 Players and take turns on the same screen. Player 1 is ✖️, Player 2 is ⭕.
Tip
Taking the centre square first is the strongest opening. Corners are next best.
