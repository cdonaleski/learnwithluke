How to Play

Moving
Arrow keys or W A S D. On a tablet, swipe across the maze or use the arrow buttons. You can't walk through walls, so a move that doesn't work means there's one in the way.
Your trail
Squares you've already visited glow faintly, which makes it much easier to spot the parts you haven't tried yet.
Stuck?
💡 Show the Way draws the route from where you're standing to the flag. It doesn't cost anything — but the timer keeps running!
Every maze has an answer
Mazes are carved so that every square can be reached and there's exactly one route between any two squares. So there are no loops to go round in, and no dead ends that aren't really dead ends.
Tip
Try keeping your hand on one wall — always turning the same way at every junction. In a maze like this, following one wall will always get you out eventually.
