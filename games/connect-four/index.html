🔴🟡 Connect Four

Drop your discs and get four in a row. Across, down or diagonally — they all count. Discs always fall to the bottom, so think about what you're building underneath.

How to Play

Dropping a disc
Tap the ▼ above a column, or press 1–7. Your disc falls to the lowest free space in that column.
Winning
Four of your discs in a straight line — across, down, or either diagonal. The winning four light up.
Computer skill
Easy mostly plays at random. Medium looks a few moves ahead but slips now and then. Hard looks six moves ahead and does not slip — it will spot a trap you set two turns before it closes.
Two players
Switch to 👫 2 Players and take turns on the same screen. Red goes first, and who starts swaps each round.
Tip
Play in the middle column when you can. A disc in the centre can be part of more winning lines than one at the edge, which is why the computer grabs it early.
Watch out
Before you drop, look at what your disc puts underneath your opponent — filling a square can hand them the space right above it.
