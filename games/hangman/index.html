How to Play

Guessing
Tap a letter, or just press it on a keyboard. If the word contains that letter it appears everywhere it belongs — a letter that turns up twice costs you only one guess.
Wrong letters
Six wrong letters and the round is over. Used letters turn green if they were in the word and grey if they weren't, so you never waste a guess repeating yourself.
Stuck?
Press 💡 Hint for a clue about what the word means. It doesn't cost you a guess.
Categories
Animals, Food, Space, School and Nature. Your choice is remembered next time, and words won't repeat straight away.
Tip
Start with vowels — A, E, I, O, U — then the common consonants like R, S, T, L and N. Most words hide fewer places than you'd think once the vowels are showing.
Grown-ups: adding words
Open games/hangman/js/words.js and copy one of the blocks. Each entry needs a word and a hint. Words must be letters only — no spaces or apostrophes — since the keyboard only offers A–Z.
