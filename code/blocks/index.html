    How to Play

    Building a program
    Tap a block on the right and it joins your program on the left. Tap the ✕ on a block to take it away. Then press Run and watch the robot follow your instructions, one at a time.
    Putting blocks inside a loop
    Tap a 🔁 Repeat block and it opens up — everything you tap next goes inside it, and appears indented. Press ⬅ Out of the loop when you want to add blocks after it instead. Use − and + to change how many times it repeats.
    Stars
    Getting the treasure is a win. Getting it in the fewest possible blocks is ⭐⭐⭐. The "best possible" number is always shown, so you know what you're aiming for.
    Bonk!
    If the robot walks into a wall it stops and shakes. Look at where it got stuck — that's the part of your program to fix.
    The big idea
    The robot only does exactly what you tell it, in exactly the order you say. When it goes wrong, the robot isn't being silly — the instructions were. Finding out which one is the whole job of programming.
