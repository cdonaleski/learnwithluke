What we promise

The site stays free
Every game, tool and lesson is free and always will be. Nothing is held back for people who chip in, and nothing here nags a child to buy anything.
No adverts
There are none, and there will not be any. Advertising to children is the thing this site is deliberately built to avoid.
Nothing is tracked
No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no cookies. Scores and progress are saved in your own browser and never leave your device — which is also why they do not follow you to another one.
The music player
The music and video players are only loaded if you press play. Until then nothing is requested from Spotify or YouTube and nothing is set on your device. That is a deliberate choice: an embedded player normally starts tracking the moment a page opens. The videos also use YouTube's no-cookie player rather than the ordinary one.
Links that leave the site
The buttons above open other companies' websites in a new tab, and those have their own rules about data. We do not pass anything to them about you, but once you are there you are on their turf — worth knowing before handing a tablet back to a child.
Where donations go
Straight into running costs — the domain, and the time to build the next thing. It is a small site made by one family, not a company.
