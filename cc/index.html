What Memory Master Means

It is recitation, not a quiz
Someone says the prompt and you say the whole answer back, out loud, from memory. No multiple choice, no nearly. So the practice page has two kinds of mode: Cards and Match you can do alone, and Recite, which needs a parent or a friend holding the screen to check you.
All of it, in one go
Every section of every strand — the tables, the timeline, the Latin, the science, the English, the geography, the history. The proof is given in four passes: to a parent, to another parent, to your tutor, and finally to the director.
Weeks are the unit
The twenty-four weeks of the Year at a Glance are the same numbers as the gray boxes on the proof sheets, so the practice page is built around them: what you drill is shaped like what you will be asked. It can also hand you one day's worth — a slice chosen by the day of the week and whether the date is even or odd, so you do a little every day and still come round to the whole year.
A note on the history sentences
The history sentences are Classical Conversations' own writing, from the Foundations Guide — © 2018 Classical Conversations® MultiMedia, Inc. They are shared here word for word for educational use only, not for resale or profit, and checked against the printed proof sheets.
