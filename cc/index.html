What Memory Master Means

It is recitation, not a quiz
A tutor says the prompt and you say the whole answer back, out loud, from memory. No multiple choice, no nearly. That is why this page has a Recite mode where the answer stays hidden until you have already said whether you knew it.
All of it, in one go
Every section of every strand — the tables, the timeline, the Latin, the science, the English, the geography, the history. The proof is given in four passes: to a parent, to another parent, to your tutor, and finally to the director.
Sections are the unit
The numbered gray boxes on the proof sheets. The practice page is built around them for exactly that reason, so what you drill is shaped like what you will be asked.
A note on the history sentences
They are Classical Conversations' own writing, from the Foundations Guide they sell, so they are not published on this site. Everything else here — the arithmetic, the Vulgate, the capitals, the parts of the body — is plain fact that belongs to nobody.
